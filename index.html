Pip Jarvis Kids Studio

Pip Jarvis Kids Studio is a small personal tool that makes original, animated kids videos starring Pip the blue bunny. It helps one creator send finished videos to their own TikTok account as drafts, so they can check each video before posting it.

What it does

The creator signs in with TikTok and approves access. The tool then sends a finished video, with a title and hashtags, to the creator's TikTok inbox. The creator opens TikTok, reviews it and chooses whether to post.

Who it is for

The tool is used only by its owner, to manage their own account. It does not post to anyone else's account and has no public sign-ups.
