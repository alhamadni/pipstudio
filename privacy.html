Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Pip Jarvis Kids Studio ("the app") does with information.

What we access

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and the owner's TikTok open ID. It uses them only to send videos to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts.

Where information is kept

The token and open ID are stored on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database. Videos are made on that computer and sent straight to TikTok.

What we do not do

We do not sell, share or publish any information. We do not collect information about other people, and we do not show advertising. The videos are made for children, and the app does not collect any data from children or from viewers.

Your choices

The owner can remove the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and websites. Deleting the stored token file from the computer also ends access.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
